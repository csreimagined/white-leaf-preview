AT WHITE LEAF BOUTIQUE IN BRIGHTON

For the past 20 years, we’ve been creating unforgettable wedding dress shopping experience in the heart of vibrant North Laine, Brighton. Our goal is to provide every bride with a personalised, one-on-one consultation in our spacious dressing room. This way, we can help you find the perfect wedding dress that matches your unique personality and style.

We offer appointments from Monday to Saturday, including two late evenings.

To book an appointment, please select your chosen date below,

Do not hesitate to get in touch via email – simply click on boutique@whiteleafbridal.com or if you would like to speak to us directly and check our availability for last minute appointments, please call us on 07947 601 168 and we will be happy to book your appointment over the phone.

We look forward to welcoming you to our boutique and helping you create beautiful memories that last a lifetime.

APPOINTMENT TIMES

Our boutique caters for only one bride at a time and our 90 minute appointments are unhurried and focused upon you. We have set appointment times on Saturdays, during the week the appointment times can be adjusted to suit you.

APPOINTMENT FEES

Saturdays are a popular choice for bridal appointments, and we want to ensure that you have a truly special experience when you visit us. To provide each of our brides with the dedicated attention and time they deserve, we only take a limited number of appointments per day. This means that our availability is limited and we require a non-refundable appointment fee of £25 to secure your booking. The fee is fully redeemable against the purchase of your wedding dress.

Weekday appointments are free, while Saturday bridal appointments carry a booking fee.

GUESTS

At White Leaf Boutique, we believe in providing a personalised and intimate experience for each of our brides. To ensure that we can dedicate our time and attention to you, we kindly request that you bring a maximum of three guests to your appointment.

We find that smaller appointments allow us to guide you through our collection of dresses and discuss any concerns or ideas you may have in a comfortable and relaxed atmosphere. However, if you would like to bring more guests, we will do our best to accommodate your request and make your experience as enjoyable as possible.

PHOTOGRAPHY

We politely request that you do not take photographs during your appointment. This is not to stop you having pictures, but to protect the quality of images. We are more than happy to take pictures of you in your favourite dress from the best angles and in the best lighting, we will forward them to you after your appointment.

STREAMING YOUR APPOINTMENTS

We understand that not all loved ones can be present for your appointment. We offer virtual appointments via Zoom, Face Time, or video call so that your family and friends can still share the experience with you.
