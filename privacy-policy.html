PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

At White Leaf Boutique, we are strongly committed to protecting the privacy of our website visitors and customers. This Privacy Policy explains in detail our practices regarding the personal information we collect, use, share, and store about you.

Information Collection

We collect certain personal information when you use our website, make purchases, contact us, sign up for our mailing list, or provide information through online forms. The types of information gathered may include your name, mailing address, email address, phone number, payment information, comments, inquiries, and browsing activities on our site. Credit card details are collected securely through encrypted payment processing when purchases are made. We gather this information to improve our services, process orders and requests, administer contests and surveys, analyse usage trends, personalise your experience on our site, and keep you informed through email campaigns about products and services we think may interest you. Information is collected directly when provided by you and automatically through cookies and website analytics tools.

Use of Information

We use the information gathered primarily to deliver, support, and improve your customer experience. This includes processing transactions, fulfilling orders, responding to inquiries, customising and enhancing our services, sending communications you have requested, notifying you of changes to our offerings, evaluating website performance, ensuring site security, and providing a personalised shopping experience. Credit card details are used solely to securely process payments and are not stored on our servers. We will not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes without your consent.

Service Providers

We partner with third-party service providers to process payments, fulfil orders, deliver packages, provide customer service, host our servers, email distribution, database management, and other services. These providers have access to customer information needed to perform their functions but are prohibited from using it for other purposes. Your payment information is only processed by our PCI-compliant third-party processor and not accessed or stored by White Leaf Boutique.

Data Transfers

In certain circumstances, we may transfer your personal data to other parties as part of a corporate sale, reorganisation, or other business transition, to enforce our policies, protect our rights, or comply with legal requirements. International transfers may occur to provide requested services. In these cases, we will take reasonable measures to ensure your information is kept secure and used only in line with this Privacy Policy. If you utilise our services from outside the EU, your data may be transferred internationally to provide support.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to request a copy of personal information we hold about you. Simply contact us to make this request. If any of your details change or you believe we have inaccurately recorded information about you, you can notify us to have this corrected or removed. Marketing emails you receive include an unsubscribe link allowing you to opt-out of future communications. You can also contact us directly to update your preferences. Please be aware that certain communications, such as order confirmations, are necessary for transactional purposes and cannot be opted-out of.

Data Retention

We retain collected information for the period necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy unless a longer retention period is required by law. Personal data related to marketing opt-ins and website usage is kept until you unsubscribe. Payment details are not stored after the transaction is processed.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store information related to your site usage. We use cookies to optimise your browsing experience, facilitate account login, enable site features, gather broad demographic usage data, and display relevant promotions. Cookies do not give us access to your computer or reveal personal information about you. Most browsers allow controlling cookies via their settings. Limiting cookies may impact site functionality.

Security Safeguards

We are committed to diligently protecting the personal information of our customers and website visitors. We employ industry standard security measures and technologies like encryption, firewalls, SSL, and access controls to secure and safeguard all private data collected through our website and stored in our databases.

Updates to Privacy Policy

We may occasionally make changes to this Privacy Policy as business needs and practices evolve. Any revisions will take effect immediately upon posting the updated version on our website. Your continued use of our services indicates acceptance of the modified terms. Please check back periodically for updates.

If you have any questions about our privacy practices or handling of your personal information, please contact us at boutique@whiteleafbridal.com.
