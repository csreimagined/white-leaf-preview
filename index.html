Find your perfect wedding dress

Welcome to White Leaf Boutique, where modern meets bohemian in the heart of vibrant North Laine in Brighton & Hove. Our boutique offers a fresh, friendly and personal wedding dress shopping experience for the free-spirited and fashion-forward bride.

Our collection of wedding dresses is inspired by the latest trends and bohemian style, offering a unique blend of classic and modern designs. From flowy silhouettes to daring cuts, our gowns are perfect for the bride who wants to make a statement on her special day.

We believe that a wedding dress should be a reflection of your individuality and style. That’s why we offer a range of gowns that are designed to enhance your natural beauty and make you feel confident and radiant on your special day.

From the first fitting to the final touches, we will work with you to create a wedding day look that’s as unique as you are. We offer a personalised service, ensuring that any alterations you choose suit your body and your vision.

Whether you are planning a traditional, modern, beach, rustic, outdoor, bohemian, whimsical, or elopement wedding, visit us at White Leaf Boutique to discover our carefully curated selection of modern and bohemian wedding dresses that are both affordable and stylish. Let us help you make your wedding day unforgettable.
