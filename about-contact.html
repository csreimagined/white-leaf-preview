ABOUT US

At our bridal boutique, we pride ourselves on having 20 years of experience in the industry. During the first decade of our business, we focused on designing and manufacturing wedding dresses, which has given us a unique perspective and expertise in garment design and production. With this knowledge, we are able to provide our clients with unparalleled guidance and support as they navigate the process of finding the perfect wedding dress. Our commitment to quality and attention to detail ensures that every bride who walks through our doors feels confident and beautiful on their special day. After a decade of designing and manufacturing bespoke wedding dresses, our founder Blanka had to make the difficult decision to change the structure of the boutique due to the demands of raising two boys. Since then, we have shifted our focus to curating a carefully selected collection of wedding dresses from leading designers that fit seamlessly with our own aesthetics. We take great care to ensure that each of the designers we stock shares our commitment to responsible sourcing and ethical production. While our approach may have evolved, our passion for helping brides find the perfect dress remains as strong as ever.

With over 20 years of experience in the bridal industry, we are confident in our ability to guide you towards the dress of your dreams, and our long-standing commitment to quality and service has earned us a great reputation among our brides, who often recommend us to their friends, colleagues, and family members.

ACCESSORIES

The finishing touch to any bridal look is the addition of accessories. These small details can transform a dress and add a personal touch that reflects your unique style. At White Leaf Boutique, we understand the importance of finding the perfect accessories to complement your dress. We offer a wide range of options, including veils, tiaras, hair pieces, earrings, cardigans, and more. We would love to help you select the perfect pieces to complete your look. We also offer styling appointments that can be combined with your first fitting, so you can try on your dress with the accessories we stock and make any necessary adjustments to achieve the perfect fit.

ALTERATIONS

Every bride is different, and every gown is unique, so alterations are priced separately from your dress. There are three options, and you’re welcome to ask us which suits you best.

1. No alterations

Take your dress as purchased. No fittings or alteration work are included.

2. Arrange your own alterations

Use an independent seamstress of your choice, and arrange everything directly with them, including work, price, fittings and timings. We’re happy to recommend a seamstress if you’d like.

Your purchase with White Leaf is complete once your dress is fully paid for and collected. White Leaf isn’t part of this arrangement and isn’t responsible for the work, timing or quality.

3. In-house alterations package: £425

White Leaf manages everything for you. Anna carries out the work, your fittings take place at the boutique, and we look after the process from start to finish.

Included: one major alteration, two minor alterations, and up to two fittings.

One major alteration, for example

  • Taking the bodice in or letting it out
  • Reshaping the side seams
  • Hemming a skirt with up to 3 layers
  • Creating a bustle
  • Shortening or reshaping sleeves

Two minor alterations, for example

  • Shortening or repositioning straps
  • Adjusting bra cups
  • A small waist or hip adjustment
  • Adjusting a zip or fastening
  • Adjusting a modesty panel or lining

Additional work

Anything beyond the package is quoted before work starts, for example:

  • Hems with more than 3 layers, or complex lace work
  • Major restructuring, or resizing by more than two dress sizes
  • New sleeves, corset work, or heavy beading repair

Smaller jobs

If you only need a small alteration, we’ll quote it individually.

Additional fittings

Each extra fitting is £45. Some gowns need more than two fittings. Rush or out-of-hours fittings are quoted in advance.

When to book

Please book your first fitting at least 8 weeks before your wedding.

Payment

A non-refundable 50% deposit of £212.50 is due at your first fitting to secure your booking. The balance is due at your final fitting or collection.

DRY-CLEANING AND WEDDING BOXES

At White Leaf in Brighton, we understand that your wedding dress is one of your most treasured possessions. Whether you choose to store your dress on top of a wardrobe or under the bed, our boxes will keep it safe and secure.​
